Excalibur

I built Excalibur during my internship at Digital NEST, where Huddle AI gave us access to their APIs. The idea was simple — I wanted an AI assistant that could actually see and interact with my desktop, not just chat in a text box.

Excalibur listens for a wake word or button press, transcribes what I say using Vosk, and then a GPT-4.1 agent figures out how to get it done. It can take screenshots and read the screen using Huddle's Vision API and H-OCR, browse the web with Playwright, open projects, manage files — basically anything I'd normally do by hand. It runs as a systemd service so it's always listening in the background.

It was featured in a demo day presentation, which you can see in the LinkedIn post above.
